Partnership opportunities

Multiple ways to make the partnership visible and useful.

Every package can be adjusted around the event, available inventory, campaign goals, and the type of value your brand wants to create.

01 / BROADCAST

Broadcast Integration

Place the brand naturally inside the live viewing experience.

  • Opening and closing billboards
  • Lower thirds and logo placements
  • Replay, segment, or feature sponsorship
  • Announcer acknowledgments
  • Branded graphics and transitions
02 / VENUE

On-Site Visibility

Connect the partnership to spectators and participants at the event.

  • Video-wall placements
  • Branded audience messaging
  • Interview or stage integration
  • Activation support
  • Product visibility where appropriate
03 / CONTENT

Content & Social

Extend the partnership beyond the live broadcast window.

  • Branded highlight edits
  • Short-form social clips
  • Partner recap content
  • Behind-the-scenes integration
  • Campaign-ready event assets
04 / SUPPORT

Technical Partnerships

Show how products and services help make difficult live productions possible.

  • Equipment and infrastructure support
  • Technology-in-use storytelling
  • Field testing and demonstrations
  • Hospitality or travel support
  • Custom in-kind partnerships

How partnerships are built

A straightforward path from conversation to activation.

The goal is to match the right brand, event, audience, and inventory—then make the plan clear before production begins.

1

Discover

Learn the brand goals, audience, timeline, budget, and type of partnership being considered.

2

Match

Identify the events, placements, content, and production needs that create the strongest fit.

3

Build

Create a defined package with deliverables, responsibilities, usage, and activation details.

4

Deliver

Execute the partnership and organize the agreed assets or recap materials after the event.
