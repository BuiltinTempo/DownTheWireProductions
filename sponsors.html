Down The Wire Productions · Partnership Philosophy

Ways to partner

Show up where your support matters.

Each program can be shaped around one event, a series, a technical deployment, or a longer relationship across Down The Wire productions.

01 / FEATURE PARTNER

Broadcast & Presenting Partner

Own a highly visible position in the live show and audience-facing presentation.

  • Opening and closing billboards
  • Presented-by integration
  • Segment, replay, or feature ownership
  • Lower thirds, transitions, and announcer acknowledgments
02 / INFRASTRUCTURE PARTNER

Power the Production

Help solve the real technical problems behind remote and field-based coverage.

  • Power, connectivity, fiber, networking, and communications
  • Equipment and technology support
  • Technology-in-use storytelling
  • Field testing and behind-the-scenes integration
03 / EXPERIENCE PARTNER

Build the Venue Experience

Improve what spectators, athletes, staff, and guests experience on site.

  • Video-wall and venue-screen integration
  • Hospitality, travel, transport, and crew support
  • Interview, stage, and audience moments
  • Product sampling or activation support where appropriate
04 / CONTENT PARTNER

Keep the Story Moving

Build campaign-ready content around the event, athletes, and production itself.

  • Branded highlights and short-form edits
  • Partner recap assets
  • Behind-the-scenes and technical storytelling
  • Custom concepts aligned with campaign goals

Why the model works

The brand becomes part of the work—not an interruption to it.

The most credible partnerships fit naturally into the production. A power partner can keep remote nodes running. A connectivity partner can link the mountain. A camera or communications partner can show tools performing under real conditions. A presenting partner can help bring the entire event to the audience.
