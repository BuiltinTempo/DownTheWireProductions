Show builds

A pile of equipment is not the same as a finished production system.

The show-build service turns selected equipment into a documented, tested workflow. We can prepare the system before load-in, support deployment on site, and provide the EIC who owns the technical picture through final strike.

  • System design around cameras, formats, signal paths, recording, streaming, audio, comms, and redundancy
  • Prewire, labeling, configuration, firmware checks, and bench testing
  • Technical oversight, troubleshooting, and crew handoff
  • Practical signal-flow notes, assignments, deployment guidance, and strike expectations
