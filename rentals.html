Show builds

A pile of equipment is not the same as a finished production system.

The show-build service turns the selected equipment into a documented, tested workflow. We can prepare the system before load-in, support the deployment on site, and provide the EIC who owns the technical picture from first connection through final strike.

01

System design

Define camera counts, formats, signal paths, control positions, recording, streaming, audio, comms, and redundancy.

02

Prewire and testing

Build racks, label connections, configure devices, verify firmware, and test the workflow before it reaches the venue.

03

EIC oversight

Provide a clear technical lead responsible for commissioning, troubleshooting, crew handoff, and system stability.

04

Documentation

Create practical signal-flow notes, equipment assignments, deployment guidance, and strike expectations for the crew.
